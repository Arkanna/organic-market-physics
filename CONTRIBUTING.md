# Contributing to Organic Market Physics

Fork the project, change a rule, and show what emerges.

1. Open a Mutation Proposal issue describing the hypothesis and intended behavior.
2. Work in your fork. Keep simulations runnable without credentials, external services or real contact data.
3. Submit a pull request describing the rule change, expected behavior, before/after observations and a short screenshot, video or GIF if possible.
4. Add your fork or experiment to [EVOLUTION.md](EVOLUTION.md) so other researchers can find it.

All pull requests are reviewed. No automatic integration of untrusted code. By contributing, you agree to license your contribution under the repository's AGPL-3.0-only license.
