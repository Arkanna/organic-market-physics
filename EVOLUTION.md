# Evolution Registry

An opt-in directory of community mutations, forks and experiments. Forks do not need to register, and the project owner does not automatically receive private derivatives.

| Variant | Author | Fork/repository | Mutation | Evidence | License | Upstream PR |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline community lab | Arkanna | [Original](https://github.com/Arkanna/organic-market-physics) | Random drift, collisions, immunity, mock preparation orders | [Play locally](README.md) | AGPL-3.0-only | — |

## Possible experiments

- New stochastic motion laws
- Attraction or repulsion based on interests and offer fit
- Time-dependent immunity
- Cooperative and competitive agent strategies
- Reproducible seeds and experiment comparisons
- Consent-aware thresholds for outreach preparation

Open a Mutation Proposal issue or submit a pull request adding a row to this registry. Credit the original author of each mutation.
